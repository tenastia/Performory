Interactive prototype

Performory

A memorization companion for musicians. Every screen is live — upload a score, pick sections, work through fragments, record a take.

Walk the flow

  1. 01Practice a piece. Tap a card, then Memorize, choose sections, Start Practice.
  2. 02Record yourself. The mic on the practice screen captures a real take. Needs microphone permission.
  3. 03Add a score. New Piece → From Device. Pick a photo and it becomes the artwork.
